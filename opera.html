How to play

  • Bring the figure exactly inside the dashed outline.
  • Every drawn square is a button, but it only works while the figure stands on it: the lit ones are the pale symbols, on the body of the figure.
  • The arrowheads slide it: one head one square, two heads two, three heads three.
  • The curved arrow turns the figure a quarter turn — or a half turn — around the square you press.
  • The dashed axis flips the figure; the axis runs through the square you press, and may be slanted.
  • Dark squares are walls: the figure can't land on them, and can't pass through them — not even by turning.
  • Amber squares are fire: the piece of the figure that comes to rest on one burns away for good. Sometimes you must keep clear of it — and sometimes the outline is smaller than the figure, so burning is the only way to fit.
  • A move that would take the figure off the board can't be made.
  • The two arrows under the board undo and redo your moves: you can try things without fear.
  • Finishing a level earns one to three stars: three in the fewest moves, two within four times that. Both targets are shown at the top.
  • Stars unlock later levels, so a level you can't crack never blocks your way. Skip walks away to the next one — as long as that one is already open.
